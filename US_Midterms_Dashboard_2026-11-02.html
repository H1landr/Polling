01How the forecast moved

Thirty days of daily published probabilities. The House eased from 86% to 83% in the final week. The Senate kept climbing to the close — 50% on 2 October to 59% today on Call the Map, and 83% to 88% on On Point in the last two days alone.

02Michigan and South Carolina — the last 48 hours

Two races moved more than any others in the final weekend, in opposite directions and for reasons that have almost nothing in common. One is a story about a candidate surviving a defection; the other is a story about a name losing its grip.

04Senate — the only open question

Democrats need a net +4 for 51 seats, or +3 to reach 50 and rely on the vice presidency. On current averages they get exactly four — North Carolina, Maine, Texas and Ohio — and lose none.

Five races sit inside 1.5 points: Kansas, Alaska, Iowa, Maine and Texas — with Ohio at 1.7. Win three and Democrats reach 50; win four and they reach 51 without the White House. Everything else on the Senate map is settled to within polling error.
